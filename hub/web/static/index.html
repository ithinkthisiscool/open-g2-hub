Where do I find the link?
Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → "Secret address in iCal format".
Apple iCloud: Calendar app → share the calendar → Public Calendar → copy the link (webcal:// works).
Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → copy the ICS link.
Treat the link like a password: anyone with it can read your calendar. Synced events are read-only here.

Custom news article

Push one article with your own title, source and text (replaces the news on the glasses until the next refresh).

News FIFO reset

Sends AppResetClearAllDataMsg (cmdId 13) to wipe the glasses' news slots. Every news push already starts with it; use this when the glasses show stale news from the Even app.

Even AI manual steps

SID 0x07, one step at a time. Set Apps → Even AI to "Off" first, or the automatic flow runs too. Say "Hey Even", then within about 3 s press ENTER → ASK → REPLY. (CONFIG is captured but its effect is unconfirmed.)
